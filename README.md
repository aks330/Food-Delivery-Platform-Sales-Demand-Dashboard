# Food Delivery Platform Sales & Demand Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to compare the sales performance and demand patterns of two food delivery platforms, Swiggy and Zomato.

The dashboard analyzes more than 10,000 records covering April 2025 to March 2026, providing insights into orders, revenue, profit margin, customer ratings, and platform performance.

## Project Objectives

- Compare the sales performance of Swiggy and Zomato.
- Analyze order volume and revenue trends.
- Evaluate profit margin and average customer ratings.
- Identify top-performing cities and food categories.
- Compare performance across different payment modes.
- Present business insights through interactive visualizations.

## Dataset

The project uses food delivery data from April 2025 to March 2026, containing more than 10,000 records.

The analysis covers platform performance, order information, revenue, profit margin, customer ratings, city, food category, and payment mode.

## Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report contains two pages:

1. **Sales Overview** — Summary of key performance indicators and overall sales performance.
2. **Platform Comparison** — Comparative analysis of the two food delivery platforms across relevant business dimensions.
